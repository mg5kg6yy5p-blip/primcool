GCT Output-Tax Report

Gross output tax, accrual basis. This is the GCT you charged customers on invoices issued in the period — a liability held on behalf of TAJ, not revenue. It is recognised on the invoice issue date (whether or not the customer has paid yet) and excludes input-tax credits (GCT you paid on purchases), which the system does not track. Subtract your own input tax before filing. Drafts and cancelled invoices are excluded.
